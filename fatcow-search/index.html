FatCow Icon Search

FatCow Icons (also known as "Farm-Fresh Web Icons") is a collection of 3,926 free icons originally created by FatCow Web Hosting. The set was first released in the late 2000s to provide web developers and designers with a comprehensive, consistent icon library. Each icon is hand-crafted in pixel art style and available in 16×16 and 32×32 pixel sizes, in both colored and greyscale variants. The collection covers a wide range of categories including business, nature, technology, communication, commerce, and more, making it one of the most complete free icon sets of its era.

The icons were later open-sourced and are now maintained on GitHub. They remain popular for prototyping, internal tools, and retro-styled interfaces.
